How to find your eSIM number (ICCID)

The ICCID is your eSIM’s own number, usually 19 or 20 digits long. You need its last 6 digits to check how much data is left on our Check my data page.

Easiest: check your eSIM email

Open the PDF attached to your eSIM email from eSIMs Cloud (get@esims.cloud). Your ICCID is printed at the top of the first page, and it’s also in the file name (eSIM- followed by your ICCID, then .pdf).

Or find it in your phone

  • Your phone shows the ICCID once the eSIM is installed.
  • If your phone has more than one SIM or eSIM, make sure you look at your travel eSIM (for example the line you named “Travel”).

iPhone

  1. Open Settings › General › About.
  2. Scroll down to the section with your travel eSIM’s name.
  3. Your ICCID is listed there.

Don’t see it? The line may be switched off. Go to Settings › Cellular, tap your travel eSIM, turn on Turn On This Line, then check About again.

Samsung Galaxy

  1. Open Settings › Connections › SIM manager.
  2. Tap your travel eSIM.
  3. Tap More information if you see it. Your ICCID is shown there.

Google Pixel

  1. Open Settings › Network & internet › SIMs and note whether your travel eSIM is listed first or second.
  2. Go back to Settings and tap About phone.
  3. Tap SIM status (sim slot 1) or SIM status (sim slot 2), matching the position you noted. Your ICCID is shown there.

Other Android phones

Open Settings › About phone and look for Status or SIM status. Choose your travel eSIM to see its ICCID.

Found it? Check my data

Menu names can differ slightly by phone brand and software version.

FAQ

Is the ICCID the same as my phone number?

No. The ICCID identifies the eSIM itself. Most travel eSIM plans are data only and don’t come with a phone number.

Can I find my ICCID before installing the eSIM?

Yes. It’s in the PDF attached to your eSIM email. Your phone only shows it after the eSIM is installed.
