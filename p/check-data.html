Where do I find my eSIM number (ICCID)?

  • iPhone: Settings › General › About. Scroll down to your travel eSIM and look for ICCID.
  • Samsung: Settings › Connections › SIM manager. Tap your travel eSIM (then More information if shown) and look for ICCID.
  • Google Pixel: Settings › About phone › SIM status. Choose the slot of your travel eSIM and look for ICCID.
