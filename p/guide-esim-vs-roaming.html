eSIM vs roaming vs local SIM card

There are three common ways to get mobile data abroad. Here’s how they compare.

Travel eSIMRoamingLocal SIM card
SetupScan a QR code before you flyNoneVisit a shop or airport counter, often with ID
CostPrepaid: you pay a fixed price upfrontSet by your home carrier, and can be highLocal prices
Home numberStays active in your phoneStays activeRemoved while the local SIM is in
Several countriesOne regional plan can cover many countriesDepends on your carrierUsually a new SIM per country
Calls & SMSUsually data only (use WhatsApp or FaceTime)Included, at roaming ratesLocal number included

When a travel eSIM is the best choice

  • You want data as soon as you land, without hunting for a SIM shop.
  • You want to keep your home SIM in your phone for bank SMS codes and calls.
  • You’re visiting several countries and want one plan.
  • You want to know exactly what you’ll pay before you go.

When something else may suit you better

  • Your phone doesn’t support eSIM or is locked to your carrier.
  • You need a local phone number for calls.
  • Your carrier’s roaming package already covers your trip at a price you’re happy with. Check their rates before you go.

FAQ

Will I still get SMS codes from my bank?

Yes, as long as your home SIM stays on. You can keep your home line active for calls and SMS and use the travel eSIM for data. Your home carrier may charge for roaming calls and SMS.
