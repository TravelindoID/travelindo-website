Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

We collect only what we need to sell you an eSIM and deliver it: your email, your order and payment details (handled by Whop), and the eSIM itself. We never sell your data.

Who we are

Travelindo is run by Hotelmurah, Jakarta, Indonesia. Contact for privacy questions: cs@hotelmurah.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhy we need itWhere it comes from
Email addressTo send your eSIM QR code and answer support requestsYou, on our site or at checkout
Name, billing address, card detailsTo take payment and prevent fraud. We never see your full card number.You, in Whop's checkout
Order details (plan, price, order reference)To deliver and support your orderCreated when you pay
eSIM details (QR code, activation code, ICCID)To show your QR code on the thank-you page and help if it does not workOur eSIM supplier
Technical data (IP address, browser)To run and protect the websiteYour browser, via our hosting provider

Who we share it with

Only the services that make your order work:

  • Whop – payment processing and receipts.
  • Our eSIM supplier – creates your eSIM and emails you the QR code and install steps.
  • Make.com – the automation that places your eSIM order and stores the order record.
  • Cloudflare – hosts this website.

Some of these companies store data outside Indonesia (for example in the EU, US or Singapore). We share only what each service needs to do its part.

How long we keep it

Order and eSIM records: 24 months after purchase, for support and accounting, then deleted. Payment records are kept by Whop under its own policy.

Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) you can ask us to show you your data, correct it, delete it, stop processing it, or withdraw your consent. Email cs@hotelmurah.com; we reply within 14 days.

Cookies

Our site does not use advertising cookies. Whop's checkout may set cookies needed for secure payment.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
